Games Club

Five classic games in one calm app, made to be easy on the eyes: Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, Mahjong and Sudoku. Large cards and tiles, a new daily puzzle every day, no timers and no ads.

Help

  • Daily puzzle: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Today's puzzle is always free. Your streak counts the days you solve it, and one missed day a week is kept by the streak freeze.
  • Free and Club: Klondike, one-suit Spider, two Mahjong layouts, easy Sudoku, unlimited undo and 5 hints a day are free. Club adds every game and mode, every past daily puzzle, unlimited hints, themes and detailed stats.
  • Restore a purchase: Club tab → Restore Purchases. Family members get Club through Family Sharing automatically.
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Games Club.
  • Bigger text: Large mode is on from the start (Settings in the app). The app also follows your iPhone's text size.

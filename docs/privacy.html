Games Club Privacy Policy

Games Club collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it works offline.

Your games, daily puzzles and statistics are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, your streak and statistics are synced through your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage); we never see them. If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your achievements and leaderboard scores under Apple's privacy policy. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple; we receive no personal information. Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your device.
